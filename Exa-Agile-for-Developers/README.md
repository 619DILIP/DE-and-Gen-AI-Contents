# TC-Agile-for-Developers

## Overview

The intent of this material is to outline the Agile project management philosophy. Agile is an iterative approach to delivering a project throughout its lifecycle. The material introduces the concept of the Software Development LifeCycle (SDLC). It then outlines Agile methodologiy and Scrum processes. Finally, the material elaborates on specific aspects of Agile, Story pointing and Scrum Ceremonies.

The material is currently structured as one module, "SDLC," comprised of 5 topics as outlined below:

- [SDLC](./modules/001-SDLC)
  - [Introduction to SDLC](./modules/001-SDLC/001-Introduction-to-SDLC)
  - [Agile](./modules/001-SDLC/002-Agile)
  - [Project boards](./modules/001-SDLC/003-Project-boards)
  - [Story pointing](./modules/001-SDLC/004-Story-pointing)
  - [Scrum Ceremonies](./modules/001-SDLC/005-Scrum-Ceremonies)
- [Activities](./modules/999-Activities)
  - [Introduction to SDLC](./modules/999-Activities/001-Introduction-to-SDLC)
  - [Agile and Scrum Processes](./modules/999-Activities/002-Agile-and-Scrum-Processes)
